About Moonchild

On orders not to go ranging, Aneise contends with her lonely days on the edge of two worlds, loathe to cause more trouble for her maligned, but hard-working mother, Donella.

But the allure of adventure is difficult to ignore.

When her mother's work entails a whole fortnight alone, Aneise consoles herself with a small excursion in homage to her beloved spirits. Hoping to locate a relic of her favored god, she instead encounters a torment capable of destroying everything and everyone she knows.

In the wake of this torment, malicious beings haunt Aneise; her nightmares manifesting into reality. Refusing to hide or look away, she chooses to fight the malevolent forces converging upon her home and her people, too young to understand the perils of a child left to face things adults will not.
